# **App Name**: Recipe Snap

## Core Features:

- Ingredient Identifier: Use a tool to analyze a photo of food and identify potential ingredients.
- Recipe Generator: Generate a recipe based on the identified ingredients, suggesting cooking steps and potential variations. Uses a tool to decide whether to provide alternative ingredients, or steps.
- Recipe Display: Display the generated recipe in a clear, step-by-step format with an ingredients list.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Earthy green (#386641) to evoke a sense of nature and freshness.
- Secondary colors: Light beige (#F0EAD6) for a clean background.
- Accent: Burnt orange (#D4A373) for interactive elements and highlights.
- Clean and readable fonts for recipe steps and ingredient lists.
- Simple, line-based icons for different food categories and cooking actions.
- Clean and organized layout to emphasize the food images and recipe details.
